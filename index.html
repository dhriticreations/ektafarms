Our Story

Where Moments Become
Memories

Nestled on Agra Road, Aligarh, Ekta Farms is the city's most celebrated multi-function banquet destination. After a stunning renovation, the farm now boasts world-class facilities while retaining the warmth and grandeur that has made it a beloved venue for generations of families.

From lavish weddings and ring ceremonies to corporate events, birthday soirées and kitty parties — our professionally designed spaces adapt to every dream and occasion.
